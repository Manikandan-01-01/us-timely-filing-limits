# Medicare fee-for-service appeal levels (Part A and Part B)

These are the five levels for Original Medicare claim appeals, with each filing deadline and the amount-in-controversy (AIC) thresholds for calendar year 2026. All rules were checked on 2026-11-11 against the eCFR and the Federal Register.

At every level, Medicare presumes you received a notice **5 calendar days after the date on it**, unless you can show you got it later. Each level also allows a late filing if you show good cause.

| Level | Who decides | Deadline to file | 2026 minimum amount in controversy | Rule |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Redetermination | Medicare Administrative Contractor (MAC) | 120 calendar days from receipt of the initial determination (the RA or MSN) | None | [42 CFR 405.942](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-405.942) |
| 2. Reconsideration | Qualified Independent Contractor (QIC) | 180 calendar days from receipt of the redetermination | None | [42 CFR 405.962](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-405.962) |
| 3. Hearing | Administrative Law Judge (ALJ) or attorney adjudicator, Office of Medicare Hearings and Appeals (OMHA) | 60 calendar days from receipt of the QIC reconsideration | **$200** | [42 CFR 405.1002](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-405.1002), [405.1006](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-405.1006) |
| 4. Council review | Medicare Appeals Council, Departmental Appeals Board | 60 calendar days from receipt of the ALJ or attorney adjudicator decision or dismissal | None | [42 CFR 405.1102](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-405.1102) |
| 5. Judicial review | Federal district court | 60 calendar days from receipt of the Council decision | **$1,960** | [42 CFR 405.1130](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-405.1130), [405.1136](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-405.1136) |

## Amount-in-controversy thresholds

| Calendar year | ALJ hearing | Federal court | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2026 (requests filed Jan 1 to Dec 31, 2026) | $200 | $1,960 | [Federal Register notice 2025-21879, Dec 4, 2025](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2025/12/04/2025-21879/medicare-program-medicare-appeals-adjustment-to-the-amount-in-controversy-threshold-amounts-for) |
| 2027 (requests filed on or after Jan 1, 2027) | $200 | $2,000 | [Federal Register notice 2026-19016, Sept 16, 2026](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/09/16/2026-19016/medicare-program-medicare-appeals-adjustment-to-the-amount-in-controversy-threshold-amounts-for) |

The thresholds are adjusted every year using the medical care component of the CPI-U, as 42 CFR 405.1006 requires. Claims can be aggregated to reach a threshold if they meet the aggregation rules in 405.1006.

## Related rules worth knowing

- **Reopening instead of appealing.** A party can ask the contractor to reopen an initial determination or redetermination within 1 year of its date for any reason, or within 4 years for good cause ([42 CFR 405.980(c)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-405.980)). Contractors must handle clerical errors (math or computational mistakes, inaccurate data entry, and claims denied as duplicates) as reopenings rather than redeterminations (405.980(a)(3)).
- **Council timeframe.** The Council normally decides within 90 calendar days of receiving the request for review, or within 180 days for a case escalated from OMHA ([42 CFR 405.1100](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/section-405.1100)).
- This page covers Original Medicare (Parts A and B). Medicare Advantage (Part C) and Part D appeals follow different rules in 42 CFR Part 422 and Part 423, so they aren't covered here.
